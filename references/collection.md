Each catalog record has `slug`, `author`, `post_url`, `category`, `tech_tags`, `prompt`, `prompt_partial`, `skillry_url`, and media metadata. `skillry_url` shows an original beside a live remake. `post_url` points to the creator's post. The collection's examples can inspire motion choices, but they are not evidence that a particular commercial claim is true or that a specific model will reproduce an output exactly.

The repo's four catalog categories are `motion`, `explainer`, `3d`, and `interactive`. `tech_tags` include `canvas`, `svg`, `gsap`, `threejs`, `shader`, and others. The tags describe how the collection's remake was built. Roughly half the prompt fields are partial, so inspect completeness before adapting one.
